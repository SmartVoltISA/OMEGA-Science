# FOUNDATION LINK

OMEGA-Science is the scientific validation layer.

Primary bindings:
`PROVENANCE → VERIFICATION → HISTORY`

All scientific claims retain source, method, result and status. A validated result may inform Foundation reconciliation but cannot silently redefine Foundation.

Status boundary:
`OBSERVATION ≠ HYPOTHESIS ≠ RESULT ≠ INTERPRETATION ≠ VERIFIED CLAIM ≠ FOUNDATION AXIOM`
