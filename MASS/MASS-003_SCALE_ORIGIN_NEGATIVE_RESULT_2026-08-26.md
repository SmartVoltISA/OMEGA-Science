# MASS-003 — Origin of the scale: negative result

Status: NEGATIVE RESULT / boundary condition
Class: fundamental-physics research trace

## Question
Can an absolute mass scale be generated from a completely dimensionless relational/classical system without any dimensional input, while also avoiding an externally imposed mass term, length scale, time scale, energy scale, or cutoff?

## Experiments consolidated

1. Finite relational graphs can show a spectral gap, but in the simple graph test the gap scaled approximately as N^-2 and vanished as N→∞. It was therefore a finite-size effect, not intrinsic mass.
2. Adding an explicit mass term produces a stable gap, but fails the no-preinserted-mass criterion.
3. Classical nonlinear dynamics can produce amplitude-dependent characteristic frequencies. The scale then belongs to the chosen state/amplitude, not to the theory as an intrinsic mass.
4. Dynamical mass generation / dimensional transmutation can produce a finite nonzero mass scale from dimensionless coupling dynamics, but the physical scale enters through the renormalization/UV scale (or equivalently the dimensional transmutation scale Λ). Therefore it does not derive an absolute dimensional scale from literally nothing.

## Result

A completely dimensionless classical relational system does not, by itself, provide an absolute dimensional mass scale. A dimensionful reference/scale-generating mechanism is required.

This is not a proof that no deeper theory can generate scale. It is a boundary condition on the present class of models.

## Important distinction

Relative scales can still emerge. A theory may generate dimensionless mass ratios or a dynamically generated scale Λ through quantum renormalization. The unresolved question is not whether dynamics can generate a scale at all, but whether a deeper substrate can generate the full scale-setting structure without importing it as an external physical assumption.

## Current boundary

The search therefore moves one level below mass:

> What minimal structure can generate a physical scale while simultaneously generating its own effective geometry/kinematics?

Candidate mechanisms to test next: quantum anomaly/renormalization, dynamically generated correlation length, finite-information substrate, and relational models with an endogenous scale-setting transition.

## Failure controls

Do not classify as mass if the apparent gap disappears with N→∞, depends directly on amplitude/state choice, is inserted as a bare mass parameter, or disappears when the regulator/scale is removed without a well-defined continuum limit.
