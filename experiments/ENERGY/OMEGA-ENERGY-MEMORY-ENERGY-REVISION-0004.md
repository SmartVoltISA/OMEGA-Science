# Ω-ENERGY-MEMORY-REVISION-0004

Status: REFINED HYPOTHESIS / EXPERIMENTAL TARGET
Date: 2026-08-23

## Core correction

Energy is NOT defined here as memory.

Working hypothesis:

> Energy is a manifestation/consequence of the restructuring of a system's memory-bearing relations.

Memory is the retained consequence of prior state/choice. A transition changes the relational configuration; the energetic cost/transfer/dissipation is a measurable manifestation of that restructuring.

This distinction is mandatory. Do not replace it with "energy = memory".

## Working graph

```text
prior configuration
      ↓
retained state / memory
      ↓
constraints on accessible transitions
      ↓
new interaction / restructuring
      ↓
change of relational configuration
      ↓
energy transfer / work / dissipation
      ↓
new retained state
      ↺
```

## Important distinction

Energy is not assumed to be the information itself, nor the memory storage medium by definition.

The hypothesis concerns the relation between:

- retained configuration/history;
- available transition paths;
- restructuring of relations;
- work/energy transfer and dissipation.

## Independent physical consistency check

Existing literature provides compatible phenomena without proving this Ω interpretation. In driven network dynamics, finite-duration transitions can generate memory and kinetic hysteresis while preserving dissipation in the network mapping. In hysteretic systems, the area of a hysteresis loop can quantify dissipated energy, while the system's response depends on history. These are controls, not proof of the Ω hypothesis.

## Testable prediction

For systems with comparable external input energy, changing the retained relational state should change the distribution of subsequent transitions and the energy required/dissipated during those transitions.

A stronger test should measure, separately:

1. state/history before transition;
2. transition topology/configuration;
3. input work;
4. stored energy change;
5. dissipated energy;
6. resulting state;
7. repeatability after cycling.

## Falsification criteria

The hypothesis is weakened or rejected if, after controlling for state, topology and input conditions:

- transition energy is independent of restructuring;
- history does not alter accessible transitions;
- identical restructuring has systematically unrelated energetic signatures;
- observed energy transfer is fully explained without reference to the changing relational configuration in the chosen system.

## Current status

NOT PROVEN AS A UNIVERSAL PHYSICAL LAW.

Current evidence supports a narrower statement: memory/history and relational configuration can influence transition pathways, and restructuring can be accompanied by measurable work and dissipation. The stronger Ω claim remains an open research hypothesis.
